# Sources and licenses

The scene models and editable Blender source were created for this game by the procedural authoring script `art/generate.py`; see ASSETS.md. The introductory lighthouse illustration is original SVG geometry in `src/main.js`. Music and effects are original Web Audio synthesis (`src/audio.js`); no sampled songs or recordings are included. All textures are material colors or generated shader/geometry effects. No stock models or external runtime CDNs are used.

Runtime libraries and fonts are locked in package-lock.json:

- Three.js — MIT (notice shipped in `public/licenses/three-MIT.txt`).
- Manrope Variable, Assistant Variable, Cormorant Garamond — SIL Open Font License 1.1 (notices in `public/licenses/`). Fonts are served from the local build; glyph sets cover Russian, English and Hebrew.
- Vite — MIT; development/build only.
- Playwright — Apache-2.0; development/browser checks only.

The fictional Noya, miniature architecture, narrative, score, puzzle arrangement and Kikkey presentation are original project material. This inventory does not grant a separate trademark license for the Kikkey name. No third-party client, award, endorsement, or certification is claimed.
